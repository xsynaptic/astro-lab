# satteri-img-group

The plugin stamps `imageCount` and `context` on each `<ImgGroup>`, and copies `context` onto every `<Img>` child, at build time. The values rendered below are stamped, not authored. The vocabulary comes from the plugin config in `astro.config.mjs`: contexts are `grid`, `masonry`, and `carousel`, layouts are `default`, `wide`, and `full`.

`context` and `layout` are independent. A bare group falls back to `defaultContext`.

<ImgGroup>
	<Img src="/a.jpg" />
	<Img src="/b.jpg" />
</ImgGroup>

<ImgGroup layout="wide">
	<Img src="/c.jpg" />
	<Img src="/d.jpg" />
</ImgGroup>

<ImgGroup context="masonry">
	<Img src="/e.jpg" />
	<Img src="/f.jpg" />
	<Img src="/g.jpg" />
</ImgGroup>

<ImgGroup context="carousel" layout="full">
	<Img src="/h.jpg" />
	<Img src="/i.jpg" />
	<Img src="/j.jpg" />
</ImgGroup>
